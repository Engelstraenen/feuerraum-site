# FEUERRAUM – Video-Prompts (Drafts gratis, Endrender MiniMax H3 2K ≤ 15 s)

Regeln: One-Take, nur das 4K-Startbild als Referenz (bei Wan zusätzlich Endbild), Sekunde für Sekunde, keine Klischees, kein Text im Bild.

## V1 Erste Flamme (Kapitel #flamme) – Start FRA-01 (kalt), Ende FRA-02 (warm, Licht-Edit von FRA-01) – Wan 3.0, 12 s
Locked-off eye-level camera on the black steel wood-burning stove in the modern living room at blue hour, the stove stays centered the whole time, camera only creeps slowly forward a little.
0–2 s: cold, still room, three split beech logs lie inside the cold stove behind the open glass door, cool blue light from the window.
2–4 s: a man's hand in a dark knit sleeve enters from the right at stove height holding a long wooden match, strikes it on the box, the small match flame lights up warm orange on the glass.
4–6 s: the hand reaches into the stove and holds the match to the kindling under the logs, the kindling catches, small flames climb, the hand pulls out and closes the glass door.
6–9 s: the fire grows behind the glass, flames lick around the logs, warm orange light spreads over the floor plate, the wall and the sofa.
9–12 s: the whole room turns warm and golden, the window outside stays blue twilight, the fire burns steadily, the room ends exactly like the end image.
Count: exactly 3 logs, 1 match, 1 hand. Style: photorealistic, cinematic, film grain, true-to-life fire. Avoid: cuts, morphing, extra people, text, logos, christmas decor, smoke in the room.

## V2 Showroom (Kapitel #oefen) – Start FRA-03 – MiniMax H3, 10 s
Slow steady dolly shot moving sideways along the long wall of burning modern stoves in the dark showroom, the camera glides from left to right at chest height, every stove keeps burning with real flickering flames, warm reflections move across the polished concrete floor, spotlights stay fixed. 0–10 s one continuous move, no people, no cut. Avoid: morphing stoves, text, logos, smoke.

## V3 Karriere (Kapitel #karriere) – Start FRA-15 – MiniMax H3, 12 s
Vorher → Arbeit → Ergebnis. Camera locked on the apprentice's hands and the flue pipe, slow push-in.
0–3 s: the young apprentice in black work clothes and gloves holds the black flue pipe elbow above the stove outlet.
3–6 s: he lowers the elbow onto the stove outlet, presses it down until it sits tight, checks it with the spirit level.
6–9 s: he steps down, opens the stove door, places two split logs inside and lights them with a long match.
9–12 s: he closes the door, the first flames rise behind the glass, warm light falls on his face, he nods satisfied.
Count: 1 elbow pipe, 2 logs. Avoid: clichés, top hat, costume, cuts, morphing hands, text.
